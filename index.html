Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

LoungeScout does not require an account and does not collect your name, email or any other personal information.

What stays on your device. The cards you pick, your trips, your home airport, your theme and other settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them. You can erase them anytime in Settings under Your data.

Location. If you tap Near me, LoungeScout asks to use your location. It uses it once to find nearby airports and the local weather. To do this, your approximate coordinates are sent to two services: Open-Meteo (weather) and BigDataCloud (city name). We do not store your location, and we do not link it to you.

What we do not do. No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.

Links. Lounge pages link to airlines, lounge operators and Google Maps. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave the app.

Children. LoungeScout is not directed to children under 13.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
