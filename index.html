Common questions

Where can I download it?

LoungeScout is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Do I need to enter my card number?

No. You only pick which credit cards, memberships and statuses you have from a list. LoungeScout never asks for card numbers or account logins.

Which cards and memberships does it know?

The major travel credit cards from American Express, Chase, Capital One, Citi and more, plus lounge memberships like Priority Pass and airline elite statuses. We add more over time.

How accurate is the lounge information?

We check access rules and card perks regularly and show the date we last checked. Rules change often, so confirm with the lounge or your card issuer before you travel. Spot something wrong? Every lounge page has a "Report a problem" button.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. LoungeScout is designed for both iPhone and iPad, in light and dark mode.
