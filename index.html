Know your lounge before you land.

LoungeScout shows which airport lounges your credit cards, memberships and tickets open, what it costs when they don't, and plans every trip around them.

Coming soon to the App Store Free · iPhone and iPad
What it does

Your wallet, turned into a lounge map.

Add your credit cards, memberships and elite status once. LoungeScout works out where they get you in at hundreds of airports.

Find your lounge

Every lounge at the airport, with the free ones marked for you. Filter by terminal, showers, food and more.

Plan every trip

One-way, round-trip and multi-city. See your options at each airport, layovers included, and save a first choice and backup.

Get more from your cards

Travel perks for each credit card you carry, and how far your points go when moved to airline and hotel programs.

Private by design.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your cards, trips and visits stay on your device. LoungeScout never asks for card numbers.
