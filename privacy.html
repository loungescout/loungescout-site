LoungeScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

The short version

LoungeScout has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device. The only thing that leaves it is a lounge photo you choose to share.

What the app stores

The cards and memberships you pick, your trips, the lounges you log as visited, your name, your home airport, and your settings are saved only on your device. We never see them. "Delete my data" in Settings removes them, and so does deleting the app.

Sharing your card setup

If you tap Share in the Wallet, the app makes a link that lists only which cards and memberships you picked. It has no name, card numbers or other details. You choose who to send it to. Anyone who opens the link sees the list and can add the same cards to their own app.

What we do not do

We do not ask for card numbers. The app does not use your contacts, camera or microphone. It uses your location only when you tap "Check in", as described below, and it has no analytics or advertising code. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not track you across other apps or sites.

Passport and check-ins

If you tap "Check in" on a lounge, the app asks for your location once to confirm you're at that airport. The check happens on your phone. Your location is not saved and is never sent to us or anyone else. The app saves only which lounge you checked in at and when, on your phone, to give you badges. You can turn off Passport and check-ins in Settings, and "Delete my data" removes them.

Lounge photos you share

If you tap "Add a photo" on a lounge, the photo you pick is uploaded to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), along with which lounge it is for. Apple connects the upload to an anonymous ID tied to your iCloud account. We never see your name, Apple ID or email address. We review every photo before it appears. Approved photos are shown to everyone who uses LoungeScout. The app only sees the one photo you pick; it does not get access to the rest of your photo library.

If you report a photo, the report (which photo, and which lounge) is stored the same way so we can review it. If you block a contributor, that choice is saved only on your device.

To have a photo you shared removed, email us and tell us which lounge it was for. We may also remove any photo at any time.

Lounge data updates

When you open the app, it checks loungescout.app for updated lounge information and downloads it if there is any. This request doesn't include any of your information. Like any website, our host (GitHub) receives your device's IP address to deliver the file.

If you email us

"Report a problem" opens your own mail app. If you send the message, we receive your email address and whatever you write or attach. We use it only to reply and to correct lounge information. Ask us to delete it at any time.

Links to other services

Some buttons open outside the app: your maps app (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze), Google Search for terminal maps, and airport websites. Those services have their own privacy policies and may collect information when you use them.

Apple and this website

Apple may share anonymous totals with us, such as download counts and crash reports, if you have agreed to share them with developers in your device settings. This page is hosted by GitHub, which may log visitor IP addresses.

Children

LoungeScout is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Most of your data stays on your device, so you control it. To remove a photo you shared, email us. To ask about anything you have emailed us, write to the address below.

Accuracy

Lounge access rules change often. LoungeScout is a guide, not a guarantee. Check with the lounge or card issuer before you travel. LoungeScout is not affiliated with any airline, lounge or card issuer.

Contact

Questions? Email support@loungescout.app.

Terms

Sharing photos is covered by our Terms of Use.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
