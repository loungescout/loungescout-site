LoungeScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

LoungeScout has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. We do not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The cards and memberships you pick, your trips, your name, your home airport, and your settings are saved only on your device. We never see them. "Delete my data" in Settings removes them.

What we do not do

We do not collect card numbers, location, contacts, or analytics. We do not sell or share data, and we do not track you across other apps or sites.

Links to other sites

Some buttons open outside the app: your maps app (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze) and airport websites. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Accuracy

Lounge access rules change often. LoungeScout is a guide, not a guarantee. Check with the lounge or card issuer before you travel. LoungeScout is not affiliated with any airline, lounge or card issuer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
